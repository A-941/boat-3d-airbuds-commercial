## Choreography & Physical Simulation Breakdown

| Phase | Frame Range | Choreography & Physics Dynamics |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1: Gravitational Slam & Elastic Rebound** | Frames 1 – 45 | Case drops from $Z = 4.5\text{m}$ with gravitational acceleration, slams onto the obsidian mirror pedestal at Frame 22 with elastic bounce (rebounding up $12\text{cm}$ with torque), micro-shudders, ground shockwave blast, and handheld camera recoil. |
| **Phase 2: Pneumatic Spring Lid & High-Torque Ejection** | Frames 30 – 85 | High-tension spring snaps the translucent smoked crystal lid open to $-136^\circ$, mechanical damper rebound bounce at Frame 64, settling at $-130^\circ$. Both earbuds rocket out with high angular velocity, spinning across all 3 axes into zero-G apex. |
| **Phase 3: Bullet-Time Exploded Tech Reveal** | Frames 90 – 145 | Slow-motion time dilation ("Planck-rate / 10⁴³ FPS" feel). Cyber laser plane sweeps down while all 8 acoustic layers explode outward: translucent ruby silicone tips ($+0.52\text{m}$), 24K gold acoustic mesh, titanium nozzles, graphene diaphragm dome, pure copper voice coil (spins $720^\circ$ glowing hot amber), neodymium magnets, micro PCBs, and rear pods. Magnetically snaps together at Frame 142. |
| **Phase 4: Dual Soundwave Shockwaves & Dogfight Corkscrew** | Frames 145 – 235 | Earbuds face each other in center. Dual concentric sonic blast rings (Neon Cyan Sub-Bass & Neon Magenta Punch) expand radially up to $9.5\text{m}$. Earbuds dive into an aggressive double-helix dogfight corkscrew around the camera with whip-pan tracking. |
| **Phase 5: Grand Hero Presentation & Seamless Loop** | Frames 240 – 360 | Aerodynamic air-brake into symmetrical three-quarters hero presentation pose ($X = \pm 0.78\text{m}, Z = 0.88\text{m}$) displaying 24K gold boAt anchor badges, glowing cyan LEDs, translucent ruby tips, and the open crystal case with 5-bar battery equalizer HUD. Settles into docks at Frame 360 for an infinite loop. |
